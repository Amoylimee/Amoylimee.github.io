Jeremy Jiajing Chen

陈佳靖

I am a PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), with a research background in shipping, logistics, and environmental policy.

My research uses large-scale global shipping data to understand how maritime systems operate, support effective environmental policy, and facilitate the shipping industry’s transition towards a low-carbon future.

  • I apply maritime big-data analytics and machine learning to analyse vessel traffic and operational behaviour, improve shipping emissions estimation, and identify underlying patterns in maritime systems.
  • I combine shipping activity data with financial modelling to study the green maritime energy transition, particularly shipowners’ technology choices and investment decisions concerning alternative fuels and energy technologies.
  • I use econometric analysis to examine how policies influence industry behaviour and quantify the environmental, economic, and social benefits of different policy options.
