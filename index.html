Jeremy Jiajing Chen

I am a PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

My research lies at the intersection of maritime decarbonisation, quantitative shipping analytics, and environmental and transport policy. I use large-scale shipping activity data, emissions modelling, econometrics, and machine learning to study the low-carbon transition of maritime transport.
