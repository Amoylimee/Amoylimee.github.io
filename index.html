Jiajing Chen

I study how shipping data, emissions models, and policy design can support the transition towards low-carbon maritime transport.

My research lies at the intersection of maritime decarbonisation, quantitative shipping analytics, and environmental and transport policy. I use large-scale shipping activity data, emissions modelling, econometrics, and machine learning to connect analytical evidence with practical policy and industry decisions.

Current work

Selected research directions

My current work examines how the quality of shipping data affects emissions estimates, how uncertainty can be communicated transparently, and how analytical evidence can support real-world decarbonisation decisions.
